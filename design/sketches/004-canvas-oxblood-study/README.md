## Variant: Canvas · Oxblood Study

### Design stance
The canvas as *study*: literary warmth — oxblood chrome, cream paper nodes, editorial serif.
An instrumented atelier that reads like a desk lamp on old books, not a workstation.

### Key choices
- Layout: open canvas, typed artifact nodes, port-to-port typed relations, dashed labeled edges
- Typography: Charter serif titles; ui-sans chrome; mono badges
- Color: `#171112` canvas, `#e9dfce` paper, oxblood `#5a1f23` chrome, ember `#c96b4b` accent, moss evidence
- Interaction: drag nodes, drag out-port → in-port to link, double-click inspector, lens filters, zoom

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: distinctive character, fits the Substack editorial soul
- Weak at: cool-est palette family; some may find oxblood heavy over long sessions

### Best for
- A product that wants to feel like a *desk*, not a *tool*
