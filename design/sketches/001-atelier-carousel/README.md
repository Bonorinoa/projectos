## Variant: Atelier Carousel

### Design stance
The home surface as a *reading room*: current projects are objects on a warm paper strip you browse, not a dashboard you operate. Beauty first, observability on demand.

### Key choices
- Layout: horizontal carousel of 300px project cards + focus drawer (right, slide-in)
- Typography: editorial serif for project titles; ui-sans for chrome
- Color: paper `#fbfaf5` on canvas `#eeece5`, dark workshop chrome top bar, moss/coral/gold type badges
- Interaction: hover lift, arrow keys/buttons scroll, click to focus (reveals lens chips + live receipt)

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: calm, editorial, browsable; the "carousel strip" the product pitch asked for
- Weak at: density — 7 projects ≈ 2 screens; scales poorly past ~15 projects; observability is one click away, not ambient

### Best for
- The Substack-first golden path: few active projects, long-lived drafts, a room you *want* to open
