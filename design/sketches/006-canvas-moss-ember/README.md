## Variant: Canvas · Moss & Ember

### Design stance
The canvas as *field kept alive*: near-black green, warm paper, ember signals.
The instrumented atelier refined — softer shadows, rounded nodes, ambient glow on hover.

### Key choices
- Layout: open canvas; nodes rounded (14px) with soft shadows; dashed moss edges
- Typography: Charter serif titles; mono badges; understated kicker in chrome
- Color: `#0f1411` canvas, `#ece2cf` paper, moss `#152019` chrome, ember `#c96b4b` accent, mist edges
- Interaction: identical typed-artifact mechanics + ember focus ring on hover

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: living warmth without oxblood's weight; closest successor to Atelier
- Weak at: least "different" from the rejected palette — a refinement, not a departure

### Best for
- If the earlier Atelier direction felt *right* but the specific colors felt wrong
