Why evidence beats fluency

The most convincing answer is not always the one we should trust.

Generative systems are unusually good at the surface signals of expertise: rhythm, specificity, and a confident conclusion. Those signals are useful, but they are not evidence.

This changes the product question. Instead of asking whether an agent can produce a polished answer, we ask whether the surrounding system makes the answer contestable. Provenance, diffs, permissions, and failure states become part of the interface.

The best AI primitive is therefore often not a blank text box. It is a bounded action with a legible input, a reviewable result, and a durable account of what changed.
